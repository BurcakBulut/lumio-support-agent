# Updating your payment method and billing email

Workspace owners can change the card under Settings > Billing > Payment method.
The billing email (where invoices are sent) is changed under Settings > Billing > Billing email.
Changes apply to the next invoice. Past invoices are not re-sent automatically but can be downloaded again.
