# Cancelling your subscription

Owners cancel under Settings > Billing > Cancel plan, or support can cancel on request from the verified owner.
Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. The workspace keeps full access until then and is not charged again.
Cancelling does not trigger a refund; see the Refund policy.
