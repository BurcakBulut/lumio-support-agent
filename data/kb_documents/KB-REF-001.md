# Refund policy

Annual plans and Lumio Hub hardware can be refunded within 30 days of purchase.
Monthly plans and add-ons can be refunded within 7 days of purchase. Renewals count as new purchases.
After the window, refunds are not available unless a human agent grants an exception.
Hardware refunds are issued once the customer has generated a return label in the returns portal.
Refunds go to the original payment method within 5-10 business days.
