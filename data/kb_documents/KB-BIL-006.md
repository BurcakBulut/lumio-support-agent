# Duplicate or unexpected charges

A duplicate charge means the same plan was charged twice on the same day.
Duplicate charges are refunded in full, regardless of the normal refund window, to the original payment method within 5-10 business days.
Verify by checking the order history for two identical orders. If only one order exists, ask the customer for the date and amount shown on their bank statement before taking any action.
