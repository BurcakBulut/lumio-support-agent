# Billing cycles and invoices

Lumio bills Plus and Business plans monthly or annually, in advance, on the date you subscribed.
Every payment creates an invoice. Download invoices as PDF under Settings > Billing > Invoices.
Invoices are issued in EUR and sent to the workspace's billing email.
