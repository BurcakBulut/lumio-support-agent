# Plans and pricing

Free: EUR 0, up to 3 projects.
Plus: EUR 12 per month or EUR 120 per year.
Business: EUR 39 per month or EUR 390 per year. Includes SSO and audit logs.
Storage add-on (100 GB): EUR 5 per month. Lumio Hub: EUR 89. Lumio Hub Bundle: EUR 149.
All prices are in EUR.
